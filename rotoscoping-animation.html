Rotoscoping Animation

The art of rotoscoping involves tracing over live-action footage frame by frame to create realistic animation. This is the rotoscoping project using a scene from the film Deadpool & Wolverine, which features the song “Bye Bye Bye” by NSYNC.

Step by Step Procedure

Tools & Apps Used:

  • Flipaclip App
  • YouCut - Video Editor App
  • Capcut - Video Editing App
  1. Download reference video as MP4 or any video file.
  2. Download Flipaclip App.
  3. Create a "Project" in Flipaclip set to 30 FPS (Frames Per Second).
  4. Press the 3 dot button and click Add Video and choose your reference video.
  5. Trace each frame that is imported.
  6. After tracing each frame, download your created animation and download YouCut.
  7. Use YouCut/Capcut to edit the animation video, add a Picture-in-Picture (PIP) of your reference video on top of your animation video.
  8. Make sure to time your reference video with your animation video and you're done!
